We are a social club at the University of Waterloo that hosts weekly meetings on campus. Our community is open to anyone with an interest in anime, gaming, and any other related topics. We hold on-campus events, with general meetings traditionally held once a week, we typically host anime screenings, karaoke and plush cafes.

On this website you can find our contact information, learn about our mascot Emiko, and information about our club lore.
